Privacy Policy

Information on how we collect, use, and protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you use bAIsed services. This includes personal identifiers such as your name, email address, and billing information. Additionally, we collect the datasets and analysis configurations you upload for bias auditing purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services. Your datasets are processed exclusively for generating fairness metrics and identifying bias patterns as requested. We do not use your proprietary data to train our own models unless explicitly authorized.

3. Data Security and Retention

bAIsed implements robust, industry-standard security measures to protect your data. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. We may share data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform, provided those parties agree to strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer.
